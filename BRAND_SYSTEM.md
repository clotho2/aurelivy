# Aurel & Ivy — Brand & Design System Specification

## 1. Brand Nomenclature (Strict Rule)
- **Official Brand & Store Name:** Aurel & Ivy (NEVER write "Aurelivy" in customer-facing headings, body copy, or product titles).
- **Sub-brand / Legal Descriptor:** Aurel & Ivy Botanical Labs
- **Domain & Handle:** `aurelivy.com` | `@aurelivy` | `concierge@aurelivy.com`
- **Monogram:** `A&I`
- **Brand Story Hook:** *"Where Aurel (Golden Light & Cellular Radiance) meets Ivy (Enduring Botanical Longevity)."*
- **Core Positioning:** 100% Certified Vegan, ECOCERT/COSMOS Natural & Organic Clinical Anti-Aging Skincare.
